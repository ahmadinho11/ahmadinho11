### <span style="color: #4ade80">Data Systems, Analytics & High-Performance Computing</span>

</div>

I am a Data Science undergraduate at FAST-NUCES Islamabad, building at the convergence of statistical learning, low-level system design, and product-focused user interfaces. My work focuses on taking raw, unstructured data and engineering the full lifecycle around it: from deterministic low-level ingestion pipelines and reproducible NLP workflows to production dashboards and polished front-end systems.

I treat software engineering and data science as tightly coupled disciplines. Rather than writing models in isolation, I structure clean architectures that balance algorithmic rigor, memory safety, and visual clarity—ensuring that complex pipelines translate into intuitive interfaces and reliable software.

---

### Core Focus Areas

- **Predictive Analytics & NLP:** Developing modular text processing and sentiment classification pipelines using Python, Pandas, Scikit-Learn, and TensorFlow to extract structured signals from sparse or noisy text datasets.
- **Data Visualization & Business Intelligence:** Designing quantitative dashboards and automated reporting workflows with Power BI, Seaborn, and Matplotlib that turn dense operational metrics into clear decision pathways.
- **Product & UI/UX Engineering:** Crafting glassmorphic design systems and interactive prototypes in Figma, followed by responsive web implementations with modern HTML5, CSS3, and component-level architecture.
- **Systems & Algorithmic Foundations:** Implementing core data structures, concurrent process scheduling engines, and synchronized inter-process communication routines in C, C++, and x86 Assembly with strict bounds on memory and execution overhead.
